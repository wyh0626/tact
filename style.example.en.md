# Writing preferences

- Match the language of the conversation unless my draft requests another language
- Avoid exclamation marks and emoji

## Work chats

- Be brief and direct, usually one or two sentences
- Do not promise dates or results without evidence; say "Let me check" when unsure
- Skip formal greetings and unnecessary pleasantries

## Friends

- Use natural, conversational language
- A little humor is fine
